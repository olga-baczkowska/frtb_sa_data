# Rule texts

Official source: BCBS Basel Framework, MAR21, in force 1 Jan 2023, version published 23 Mar 2026:
https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/mar/21/inforce/2023-01-01/published/2026-03-23

Store here, as published and with their FAQs, the paragraphs the engine implements. Put the version date in each file:

- `mar21-04-07.md`: aggregation and correlation scenarios (MAR21.4–21.7)
- `mar21-08.md`: GIRR risk factors (MAR21.8)
- `mar21-19.md`: GIRR delta sensitivity (MAR21.19)
- `mar21-41-50.md`: GIRR buckets, risk weights and correlations (MAR21.41–21.50)

Where a local rulebook applies (for example the EU CRR), store its text as well and record the differences in `docs/methodology/`. Interpretations and elections go in the methodology, never here.
